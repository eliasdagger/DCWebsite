The Division

Human Insight.
Machine Precision.

DC Quant is our algorithmic trading division, a collaborative project between our investment team and a cohort of CS students from top Canadian universities.

We build algorithms that exploit market inefficiencies across timeframes: from intraday momentum to multi-week mean reversion. Every strategy is fully backtested before any real capital is deployed.

Our edge is the combination: domain knowledge from our fundamental analysts informing the signals our engineers code. Neither pure quant nor pure discretionary — but the best of both.
