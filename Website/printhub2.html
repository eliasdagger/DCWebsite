// FAQ
Common Questions

Still not sure? Email us at prints@printhublondon.ca and we'll get back to you fast.

🟣 Western University Area

We're based near campus and know the labs, the course deadlines, and the design standards that Western profs expect.

How do I get a quote? +
Upload your STL, OBJ, or 3MF file and our system will calculate an instant quote based on material volume, your chosen settings, and turnaround time.
What's the fastest turnaround? +
Our Rush tier guarantees 24-hour turnaround for most prints. For small, simple parts, we can often do same-day. Contact us directly for urgent requests.
Where can I pick up my print? +
We have a pickup location near Western's campus in London, ON. We also offer flat-rate $5 delivery anywhere within London city limits.
Do you offer student discounts? +
Our pricing is already student-first. Show your Western student ID for 10% off your first order. Bulk print discounts are also available for design teams and clubs.
What file formats do you accept? +
We accept STL, OBJ, 3MF, and STEP files. If your model is in a different format (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, etc.), export to one of these before uploading.
What if my print fails? +
We re-print at no charge if the failure is on our end. If there's an issue with your model file, we'll notify you before printing and work through it together.
